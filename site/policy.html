Privacy in the app

Your face

Stored on this Mac only, and never sent anywhere.

When you register your face, Andeville keeps the face data encrypted with a key held in the Secure Enclave.

The camera

Used only while Andeville is checking for your face.

That is when you register, at the lock screen, and when you open a locked app.

Your login password

Kept in the macOS Keychain, and only after you sign the consent.

Andeville has to enter your login password to unlock your Mac, so it needs to hold it somewhere safe.

Presence sensing (Labs)

The sound is not stored.

In ultrasonic mode the speakers play a tone too high to hear and Andeville listens for the echo to tell whether someone is at the desk. In AirPods mode it reads the Bluetooth signal strength of your connected AirPods.

Desk browser (Labs)

Importing needs Full Disk Access, which you grant yourself.

You turn it on in System Settings and can take it back at any time.

The limits

Andeville is not Face ID.

A MacBook has no depth camera, so a very similar face, or a photo or video, may unlock it. A liveness check is there to turn those away, and Touch ID and your password always remain as the fallback.

This website

Cookies and tracking

One cookie, and only if you sign in.

Signing in to your account sets a single session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. There is no analytics and no advertising, and the font is served from the site itself. The only third-party script is Google’s sign-in button, loaded on the Account page while you are signed out.

Your account

We keep what Google gives us to know it is you, and your licence.

Sign-in is with Google. We store your Google account’s ID, name, email address and picture link, and the licence key added to the account. We do not get your Google password. Deleting your account on the Account page removes that profile from our side; your licence key keeps working.

Links out

Other sites have their own policies.

Links to GitHub, the G.gear service site and regiontype.com take you off this site.

We use what you send only to reply.

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever is in your message.
