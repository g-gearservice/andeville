Common questions

Setting it up, using it, and fixing it.

Getting started

What do I need?

A MacBook with a notch and macOS 14 or later. Control Center in the notch needs macOS 26.

How do I set it up?

Open Andeville and its settings, then:

Allow the camera. Register your face, alone and in good light. Turn on lock screen unlock and enter your Mac password. Allow Accessibility so it can type that password for you.

Turn on “start at login” if you want it always running.

Where is my password kept?

In your Mac’s Keychain, only after you sign the consent. Your face data is encrypted and stays on this Mac.

Every day

How do I unlock my Mac?

At the lock screen, move the cursor onto the notch. If it doesn’t know you, use Touch ID or your password.

How do I lock an app?

Add it to App Lock in settings. It checks your face each time it opens. Taking it off the list needs your face too.

How do I skip a track?

Swipe with two fingers on the notch.

Where do my AirPods show?

In the notch, as soon as they connect, with the battery for each bud and the case.

How do I open Control Center?

Rest the cursor on the notch. You can set how fast it opens and closes. Needs macOS 26.

Making it stricter

It lets the wrong face in. What do I change?

Lower the match threshold, or raise the liveness threshold. Stricter settings may ask you to try again more often.

It can’t see me in the dark.

Turn on screen light. The screen glows white while it looks.

Can it stay ready at the lock screen?

Turn on stay awake. Closing the lid still puts the Mac to sleep.

Labs

What is presence sensing?

It starts looking for you when you come back to your desk. It listens for an echo of a tone too high to hear, or watches your AirPods’ signal. Nothing is recorded.

What is the desk browser?

Show the desktop and it becomes a browser behind your windows. ⌘D adds a bookmark.

If something doesn’t work

It can’t see my face.

Check camera access in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera, then register your face again.

It knows me but won’t unlock.

Allow Accessibility in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, and check you signed the Keychain consent.

How do I update?

Install the new Andeville DMG over the old one.

Is it Face ID?

No. It uses the FaceTime camera and an on-device check for photos. Touch ID and your password always work.
