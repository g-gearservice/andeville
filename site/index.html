Questions

Is this Face ID?

No. A MacBook has no depth camera, so Andeville uses the FaceTime camera and an on-device liveness model. Touch ID and your password stay as the fallback.

Can a photo of me unlock my Mac?

The liveness model is there to reject photos and screens. You can raise the match and liveness thresholds in settings.

Why does it need my login password?

macOS unlocks with your password, so Andeville enters it once it recognizes you. It is kept in the Keychain, only after you sign the consent.

Does it work without a notch?

No. It needs a MacBook with a notch and macOS 14 or later.

What is in Labs?

Experiments you switch on in settings: presence sensing with an 18–22 kHz ultrasonic echo or your AirPods signal, and the desk browser.
